About Saathly

Saathly (saath means "together") is a simple, friendly place to meet people, chat, call and share small moments, all from your browser.

Open Saathly

What you can do on Saathly

Meet & match

Swipe through people and get an "It's a match" when the interest is mutual.

Chat

Send messages and photos to your connections in private one-to-one conversations.

Voice & video calls

Call the people you connect with directly from the app.

Status

Share a photo or text that disappears automatically after 24 hours.

Groups

Create a group and talk with several friends in one place.

Saathly AI

Ask the built-in assistant when you need help using the app.

How it works

  1. Sign up with your email and a password (18+ only).
  2. Complete your profile: name, city, work/studies, languages and a short bio.
  3. Connect with people, start chatting, and invite friends to join you.

Safety & privacy

Saathly stores only what you provide: your name, email, age, city, optional photo, and your chats, calls and status. Your email is never shown to other users, and passwords are stored only in hashed form.

You can block and report any user from within a chat, and you can permanently delete your account and all your data from the Profile screen at any time.

About this project

Saathly is a personal learning and portfolio project, not a commercial service. It is provided "as is" for demonstration and testing, so please use a test email and avoid sharing sensitive personal information.

Frequently asked questions

Is Saathly free?

Yes. You can create an account and use Saathly without paying anything.

Who can use Saathly?

Saathly is for people aged 18 and above.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. You can permanently delete your account and all your data from the Profile screen. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Is my email visible to other users?

No. Your email is never shown to other users, and your password is stored only in hashed form.
