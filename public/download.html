Aapke sawal

Kya Saathly free hai?

Haan. Download karna, match karna, chat aur call karna, sab free hai.

App Play Store par kyun nahi hai?

Abhi Saathly seedha hamari website se milta hai. Isliye install karte waqt phone ek baar "unknown apps" ki ijazat maangta hai. Ye normal hai.

iPhone par chalega?

Android app abhi sirf Android ke liye hai. iPhone par Saathly web app Safari me kholo, wahan bhi chat, call aur story sab chalta hai.

Web app ka account app me chalega?

Haan, bilkul. Same email aur password se login karo. Aapki chats, matches aur photos sab wahi milenge.

Call ke liye internet kitna chahiye?

Voice call normal mobile data par chal jaati hai. Video call ke liye WiFi ya achha 4G/5G behtar rehta hai.
