Questions?

Quick answers to what people ask most.

Is Saathly free?

Yes. Downloading, matching, chatting and calling are all free.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

For now, Saathly is downloaded directly from our website. That's why your phone asks once for permission to install. It's a normal step for apps installed outside the Play Store.

Does it work on iPhone?

The app is Android only for now. On iPhone, open the Saathly web app in Safari. Chat, calls and stories all work there too.

Can I use my web account in the app?

Absolutely. Log in with the same email and password, and all your chats, matches and photos will be right there.

How much data do calls use?

Voice calls work fine on regular mobile data. For video calls, Wi-Fi or a strong 4G/5G connection gives the best quality.
