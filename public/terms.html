Privacy Policy

What we collect

Only what you provide to run the app: your name, email, age, city, optional photo, and the messages/calls/status you create.

How we use it

To operate core features (login, matching, chat, calls, status). We never sell your data, and your email is never shown to other users.

How it's stored

Passwords are stored only in an unreadable, hashed form (never in plain text). Data is stored on third-party hosting (Render, Neon, Cloudinary) used to run the app.
